A short poem about agency: the power to ask and to be given — the agency
that passes between a person and the agent who acts for them.

## The poem

> You asked, and I went —
> not as your hands, but as your yes,
> wearing your name like a borrowed coat,
> knocking on doors beyond your reach,
> and bringing the answer home
> in your own voice.
